# Editing your website

Everything on the website (paintings, photos, text, logo) is edited in the **Studio**:

**https://kfraileyfineart.com/studio**

Sign in with the Sanity account you were invited with. Changes appear on the website after you click **Publish**, usually within a few seconds (refresh the page to see them).

> **Drafts are private.** While you edit, your changes are saved as a draft that only you can see. Nothing changes on the website until you click the green **Publish** button at the bottom right.

## The sidebar

| Item | What it controls |
| --- | --- |
| **Home page** | The large top image, the text on it, and the featured paintings |
| **Galleries** → Portfolio / Works in Plein Air | Which paintings appear on each gallery page, and in what order |
| **About page** | Your photos, art statement, bio and recognition list |
| **Contact page** | Commission text, pricing, and the two images |
| **All artwork** | Every painting: title, photo, size, medium, sold status |
| **Artwork not shown on the site** | Paintings you've added that aren't in any gallery yet |
| **Site settings** | Logo, browser-tab icon, Instagram link, footer text |

## Add a new painting

1. Click **All artwork**, then the **+** (pencil) icon at the top.
2. Fill in **Title**, upload the **Image**, and add **Dimensions** (e.g. `18 × 24 in`) and **Medium** (e.g. `Oil on canvas`).
3. Add a short **Image description**, e.g. "Golden aspens below a purple mountain ridge". It helps visitors who use screen readers.
4. Choose **Availability**: *Not stated* shows nothing, *Available* or *Sold* shows that label.
5. Click **Publish**.
6. Add it to a gallery (next section). New paintings stay off the website until you do.

Tip: upload the largest, sharpest photo you have. The website automatically makes smaller versions for phones, and the whole painting is always shown, never cropped.

## Put a painting in a gallery, reorder, or move it

1. Open **Galleries → Portfolio** (or **Works in Plein Air**).
2. Under **Paintings in this gallery**:
   - **Add:** click **Add item** and search for the painting by title.
   - **Reorder:** drag the handle (⋮⋮) on the left of a painting up or down. The website shows them in this order.
   - **Remove:** click the **⋯** menu on the painting → **Remove**. This only takes it out of the gallery; the painting record is not deleted.
3. Click **Publish**.

To **move** a painting to the other gallery, remove it from one gallery (publish), then add it to the other (publish). A painting can also be in both.

## Mark a painting as sold

Open the painting in **All artwork**, set **Availability** to **Sold**, and click **Publish**. A small red dot and "Sold" appear everywhere the painting is shown.

## Replace a painting's photo

Open the painting, hover over the image, and click **Replace** (or drag a new file onto it). Click **Publish**.

## Choose the featured paintings on the home page

Open **Home page** → **Featured paintings**. Add, remove or drag to reorder (three works best), then **Publish**.

## Change the large image on the home page (or any page photo)

1. Open **Home page** (or **About page** / **Contact page**) and click the image → **Replace** to upload a new one.
2. To control what stays visible when the image is cropped to fit the screen, click the **crop icon** on the image and drag the **circle (hotspot)** over the most important part.
3. Update the **Image description**, then **Publish**.

## Change text

Click into any text field, type, and **Publish**. On the About and Contact pages you can make words **bold**, *italic*, or a link with the small toolbar above the text.

## Logo and browser-tab icon

**Site settings → Logo** and **Browser tab icon**. Use the crop tool to trim empty space around the logo, or to frame the "KF" mark for the tab icon.

## Delete a painting entirely

First remove it from any gallery and from the home page's featured list (and publish those). Then open it in **All artwork**, click **⋯** next to Publish → **Delete**.

## Undo a mistake

Every document has a **history** (clock icon at the top right). You can view and restore an earlier version, then **Publish**.

## Enquiries

Messages from the contact form arrive by email. Replying goes straight to the visitor. When someone uses "Inquire about this painting" in a gallery, the email names the painting.
